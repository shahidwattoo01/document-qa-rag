# Lumora Systems Employee Handbook

*Fictional sample document used for the Document Q&A with RAG demo.*

## 1. Working Hours and Remote Work

Lumora Systems operates on a hybrid schedule. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone, and employees are expected to be reachable on chat during that window. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule freely as long as their team's commitments are met.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams that are fully distributed may apply to their department head for a full-remote exception, which is reviewed every six months. Employees working from outside their home country for more than 14 consecutive days must get prior approval from People Operations because of tax and immigration rules.

## 2. Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, for a total of 21 days per year. PTO accrual begins on the first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their contracted hours.

Unused PTO can be carried over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire. PTO requests of five or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least 30 days in advance through the HR portal.

In addition to PTO, Lumora observes 11 company holidays per year and gives every employee two "recharge days" each year that can be taken at any time without manager approval.

## 3. Sick Leave and Parental Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is only required when an absence lasts longer than three consecutive working days.

Lumora offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents, regardless of gender, including adoptive and foster parents. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the child's arrival.

## 4. Equipment

Every new employee receives a laptop on their first day. Engineers may choose between a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop; all other roles receive a 14-inch model by default. Laptops are refreshed every three years.

Each employee also has a one-time home office stipend of $600, which can be spent on a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

## 5. Expenses and Travel

Business expenses are submitted through the Expensify integration in the HR portal. Expenses must be submitted within 45 days of being incurred. Any single expense above $250 requires a receipt and manager approval before reimbursement.

For business travel, economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours. For flights of six hours or longer, employees may book premium economy. Hotel bookings should not exceed the city rate cap published on the travel page. The daily meal allowance while traveling is $75.

## 6. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 that can be used for courses, conferences, books, and certifications. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Employees may also spend up to four hours per week of working time on approved learning activities.

## 7. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager assessment. Compensation adjustments are only made during the October cycle.
