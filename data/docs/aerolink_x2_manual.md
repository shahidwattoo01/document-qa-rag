# AeroLink X2 Smart Thermostat — User Manual

*Fictional sample document used for the Document Q&A with RAG demo.*

## 1. Overview

The AeroLink X2 is a Wi-Fi smart thermostat made by Lumora Systems. It supports heating, cooling, and heat-pump systems, learns household schedules over time, and can be controlled from the Lumora Home app.

## 2. Technical Specifications

- Display: 3.5-inch color touchscreen
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz (802.11 b/g/n) and Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 for setup
- Note: the X2 does not support 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks
- Power: 24 VAC via the C-wire; an optional power adapter kit (PAK-2) is available for homes without a C-wire
- Temperature range: 5 °C to 35 °C (41 °F to 95 °F) set-point range
- Sensor accuracy: ±0.5 °C
- Dimensions: 92 mm × 92 mm × 24 mm
- Weight: 180 g
- Supported systems: up to 2 heating stages and 2 cooling stages, or heat pumps with auxiliary heat

## 3. Installation

1. Turn off power to your HVAC system at the breaker before removing the old thermostat.
2. Take a photo of the existing wiring so you can refer to it later.
3. Label each wire with the included stickers, then disconnect them.
4. Mount the X2 base plate using the included screws and the built-in bubble level.
5. Connect the wires to the matching terminals. If there is no C-wire, install the PAK-2 adapter at the furnace control board.
6. Snap the display onto the base plate and restore power at the breaker.
7. Open the Lumora Home app and follow the on-screen pairing steps.

Installation typically takes 30 to 45 minutes. Lumora recommends professional installation for systems with more than two heating stages.

## 4. Smart Features

**Adaptive Schedule.** After seven days of use, the X2 learns your typical temperature adjustments and proposes an automatic schedule. You can accept, edit, or reject the proposal in the app.

**Eco Mode.** When Eco Mode is on, the thermostat widens the comfort band by 2 °C in both directions while the home is unoccupied. Occupancy is detected using the built-in motion sensor and the phone geofencing feature of the app.

**Filter Reminders.** The X2 tracks system run time and sends a reminder to replace the air filter after 300 hours of fan operation.

## 5. Error Codes and Troubleshooting

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|------------|
| E1 | No power on the C-wire | Check the breaker and verify the C-wire is connected at both ends, or install the PAK-2 adapter. |
| E2 | Wi-Fi connection lost | Confirm the router is broadcasting on 2.4 GHz and that the thermostat is within range. |
| E3 | Temperature sensor fault | Restart the device. If E3 persists, contact Lumora Support for a replacement. |
| E4 | HVAC system not responding | Check that the furnace door is closed and the system's own safety switch is not tripped. |
| E7 | Firmware update failed | Leave the device powered on; it will retry automatically within 24 hours. |

To restart the X2, hold the display ring for 10 seconds until the logo appears. To perform a factory reset, go to Settings > System > Reset and choose "Erase all settings". A factory reset removes the device from your Lumora Home account.

## 6. Firmware Updates

Firmware updates are installed automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. The device stays functional during the update except for a restart that lasts about 90 seconds.

## 7. Warranty

The AeroLink X2 comes with a 2-year limited hardware warranty from the date of purchase. Registering the device in the Lumora Home app within 30 days of purchase extends the warranty to 3 years. The warranty does not cover damage caused by incorrect wiring or by installation on unsupported high-voltage (line-voltage) systems.
