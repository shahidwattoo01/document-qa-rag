# Lumora Customer Support, Returns, and Service Levels

*Fictional sample document used for the Document Q&A with RAG demo.*

## 1. Support Channels

Customers can contact Lumora Support through the in-app chat, by email, or by phone. In-app chat and email are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Phone support is available Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 20:00 Central European Time.

## 2. Support Plans and Response Times

Lumora offers three support plans for business customers:

| Plan | First response time | Channels | Dedicated contact |
|------|--------------------|----------|-------------------|
| Standard | within 2 business days | email, chat | No |
| Business | within 8 business hours | email, chat, phone | No |
| Premier | within 1 hour for urgent issues, 24/7 | email, chat, phone | Yes, a named technical account manager |

Consumer customers receive Standard support at no cost. The Premier plan includes a quarterly service review with the technical account manager.

## 3. Service Level Agreement (Cloud)

Lumora guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for the Lumora Cloud API for Business and Premier customers. If monthly uptime falls below the guarantee, customers are eligible for service credits:

- Below 99.9% but at least 99.0%: 10% credit of the monthly fee
- Below 99.0% but at least 95.0%: 25% credit of the monthly fee
- Below 95.0%: 50% credit of the monthly fee

Service credits must be requested within 30 days after the end of the affected month. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 days in advance does not count as downtime.

## 4. Returns and Refunds

Hardware purchased directly from the Lumora online store can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it is in its original packaging and undamaged. Lumora provides a prepaid return shipping label for returns within the European Union and the United States.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Products bought from third-party retailers must be returned to the retailer according to their own return policy.

Opened software subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, but monthly fees are not refunded for partial months. Annual subscriptions cancelled within 14 days of purchase receive a full refund.

## 5. Warranty Claims

To file a warranty claim, contact Support with the device serial number and proof of purchase. The serial number is printed on the back of the device and is also shown in the app under Settings > About. If the claim is approved, Lumora ships a replacement device before the faulty one is returned (advance replacement). The faulty device must be sent back within 21 days, or the customer will be charged for the replacement.

## 6. Escalation

If a customer is not satisfied with how a case was handled, they can request an escalation to a support team lead. Escalated cases receive a response within 1 business day.
